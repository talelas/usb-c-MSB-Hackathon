# Artificial Intelligence Foundations

Artificial Intelligence (AI) represents one of the most transformative technologies of our time. It encompasses machine learning, deep learning, natural language processing, and computer vision.

## What is Machine Learning?

Machine Learning is a subset of AI that enables computers to learn from data without being explicitly programmed. It uses algorithms to identify patterns and make predictions.

### Types of Machine Learning:

1. **Supervised Learning**: Training with labeled data (regression, classification)
2. **Unsupervised Learning**: Finding patterns in unlabeled data (clustering, dimensionality reduction)
3. **Reinforcement Learning**: Learning through interaction and rewards

## Deep Learning

Deep Learning uses artificial neural networks inspired by biological neurons. These networks can process complex data and extract high-level features.

### Applications:
- Computer Vision (image recognition, object detection)
- Natural Language Processing (language translation, sentiment analysis)
- Speech Recognition
- Recommendation Systems

## The Future of AI

The convergence of AI, cloud computing, and edge devices creates unprecedented opportunities:
- Autonomous systems
- Personalized medicine
- Smart cities
- Advanced robotics

AI development requires careful consideration of ethics, bias, and societal impact. Responsible AI development ensures these powerful tools benefit humanity.
