Autonomous cars, or self-driving vehicles, represent a major leap forward in automotive technology. Equipped with a combination of cameras, radar, lidar, and sophisticated AI algorithms, these cars can perceive their surroundings and make driving decisions without human intervention. The goal of autonomous driving is to reduce accidents caused by human error, improve traffic flow, and provide mobility for people who are unable to drive. Companies are conducting extensive testing on public roads, and some semi-autonomous systems are already available in modern vehicles, allowing for automated lane keeping, adaptive cruise control, and parking assistance. In the future, fully autonomous fleets could transform urban transportation, making ridesharing more efficient and reducing the need for personal car ownership.
