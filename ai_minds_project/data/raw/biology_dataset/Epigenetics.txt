Epigenetics studies changes in gene activity that do not involve alterations in the DNA sequence itself. These changes are influenced by environmental factors such as diet, stress, toxins, and physical activity. Chemical markers can attach to DNA or the proteins around it, turning genes on or off. This means that lifestyle choices can directly affect how genes function. Epigenetic changes can sometimes be passed to future generations, linking environment and heredity. This field has major implications in understanding cancer, aging, and neurological disorders, and it opens new possibilities for personalized medicine.
