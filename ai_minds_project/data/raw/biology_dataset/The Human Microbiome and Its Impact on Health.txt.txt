The human microbiome is the collection of trillions of microorganisms living in and on the human body, mainly in the gut. These microbes play a crucial role in digestion, vitamin production, immune system regulation, and protection against harmful pathogens. An imbalance in the microbiome, called dysbiosis, is linked to diseases such as obesity, diabetes, inflammatory bowel disease, and even depression. Diet, antibiotics, stress, and lifestyle strongly influence the composition of the microbiome. Recent research shows that maintaining a diverse and balanced microbiome is essential for long-term health.
